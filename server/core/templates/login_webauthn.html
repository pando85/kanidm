Use your passkey

Your browser or device will ask you to approve this sign-in. The private key is not sent to Kubidm, and the credential is bound to the correct site.

Why is this harder to phish?

Kubidm and your authenticator prove the sign-in with a cryptographic challenge tied to this site. There is no reusable passkey secret for a fake page to ask you to type.
